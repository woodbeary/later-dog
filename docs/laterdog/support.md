# Support later.dog

later.dog is free and open source (Apache-2.0) and will stay that way. It runs on the subscriptions you already pay for
and on your own Cloudflare account, so the project itself has no server bill to pass on. What it does need is
maintenance: provider CLIs that move every week, inherited code to replace, releases to sign, bugs to fix.

## Two ways to help

1. **Contribute.** Pull requests are welcome; [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) says what a mergeable one looks
   like (proof over promises). Issues and feature requests go to the
   [tracker](https://github.com/woodbeary/later-dog/issues).
2. **Sponsor.** Sponsorships keep the maintainer's time on the project — and a share goes to animal rescue. The link lives
   in the app's About dialog and on the repository's Sponsor button once it is enabled; nothing in the app nags, counts
   down or gates features on it.

## Where the money goes

- Maintenance first: the time it takes to keep later.dog working against Codex, Claude Code, Grok and macOS as they change.
- A share of every sponsorship goes to organisations that shelter, feed and rehome dogs. The exact share and the
  recipients are published here when the first payout happens, with amounts — no "proceeds may" language.
- If later.dog ever offers a paid plan (metered model access the way Cursor, Cline or OpenRouter do), the same rule
  applies and is written into the plan's terms before the first invoice: a stated share of what you spend goes to animal
  rescue, and the harness still spends your tokens as frugally as it can. You should never be in doubt whether the app is
  optimising for your bill or for the margin.

## What we promise

- The ledger is public. Sponsorship totals, the share donated, and the recipients appear in this file.
- Open source stays open. No feature moves behind a paywall after the fact.
- No dark patterns. One link in About, one line in the README, that's it.
