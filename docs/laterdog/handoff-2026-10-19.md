## How images ship now

`.github/workflows/cloud-home-delta-image.yml` builds the Cloud home image as a small layer on an image Cloudflare's registry already holds (`base_tag` input), proves the result has exactly the full image's files, and runs the boot, pairing, first-trip and reaping checks. Copy the result to `registry.cloudflare.com/<account>/later-dog-home:<tag>` with `regctl image copy` (20 MB chunks; crane and `wrangler containers push` fail on large layers over slow links), then `wrangler deploy --containers-rollout=immediate`. Never PATCH a container Worker's settings.

## Known quirks

- Test homes: removing a `test-*` account and reopening it at once answers `no such table: container_schedules`; use a fresh name.
- A cold home wake restores the whole saved state from R2 (about a minute for a large home). The phone now wakes quietly behind saved chats.
