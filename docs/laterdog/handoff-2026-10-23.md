## Chats while a home sleeps (2026-10-23 night)

The home Worker now keeps a desk for each home (`deploy/laterdog/home/src/desk.ts`, live as Worker `b7033f78`):

- **Reads:** while the home runs, the last answer to each `GET /api/*` a device made (chats, accounts, routines and the
  like; never streams, sign-in steps, computers or files) is kept in R2 under `homes/<home>/desk/<device>/`. The app's page
  and files go under `desk/ui/`. While the home sleeps, a device the home has answered before gets them at once with
  `x-laterdog-saved-at`, the page keeps its security headers, and the home wakes behind it. Kept answers expire after a
  week; a 401 or 403 forgets the device and everything kept for it.
- **Sends:** `POST /api/{bots,groups}/<id>/messages` while the home sleeps is accepted at once as
  `{ ok, queued, queueId }` (202), which both apps already show as sent, kept in order in the home's Durable Object, and
  handed to the home as soon as it answers. A malformed send id is refused up front; a send the home never takes within
  an hour is dropped and logged.
- **Checked:** 175 home Worker tests, and on a sleeping test home: chats in 222 ms, a send accepted in 146 ms and
  answered 45 s later, the page with its headers, and a cookie-signed-in browser's answers kept.

Also: `ALWAYS_ON_ACCOUNTS` keeps listed homes awake (production lists the owner), and `HOME_SLEEP_AFTER` sets the idle
time (the test home uses `2m`). Models reached by API still answer through the home, so a reply to a sleeping home
waits for it to wake (about 30 s); answering those without the container is not built.
